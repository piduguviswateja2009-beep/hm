# Content to confirm before publication

- Official logo files, brand color values, and approved fonts.
- Official email, phone, WhatsApp number, hours, address and resolved Google Maps location/embed.
- Domain ownership, canonical domain, current URLs and required legacy URL redirects.
- Product catalog: own-brand vs sourced products; exact model names; specifications; applications; packaging; safety documents; availability; approved product images and alt text.
- HM Scalenor claims, including “salt-free” and “zero-maintenance”, need substantiation and approval before publication.
- Confirm service geography, exports, delivery coverage and any service/support capabilities.
- Certifications, awards, leadership biography and years-of-experience claim.
- Client names/logos and written display permission; verified testimonials and review permissions; approved case studies.
- Approved team contacts, admin user identities, and enquiry notification recipients.
- Approved educational content, datasheets/MSDS/brochures, calculators and any disclaimer text.
- Privacy contact and retention period for enquiries; legal review of privacy, cookie and terms pages.
